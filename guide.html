How to Enter Eldoria

⚜

Follow these steps to begin your part in the legend. The world is waiting. So is Freya.

Server Details
play.eldoria.net
Version: Java 1.20.x
Platform: Java Edition
Status: ● Online
Steps to Begin

Your First Steps

I
Get Minecraft Java Edition

You will need Minecraft: Java Edition to enter Eldoria. Purchase it from the official Minecraft website if you haven't already. Make sure your version is up to date — we run on 1.20.x.

II
Launch & Go to Multiplayer

Open Minecraft, navigate to the Multiplayer menu, then click Add Server. Enter the server address below exactly as shown.

play.eldoria.net
III
Enter the Realm

Click the server and press Join Server. On your first login, a cinematic introduction will play. Do not skip it — it sets the stage for everything that follows. You will receive your starting gear automatically.

IV
Meet Freya — Your Guide

After the cinematic, you will be introduced to Freya — your personal assistant and in-game guide. She will speak to you through the world, alert you to events, and point you toward what needs to be done. Listen carefully.

V
Talk to the Teleportation NPC

Leave the spawn area and find the NPC at the centre gate. They will ask where you wish to go. This is your first decision in Eldoria — choose your starting region and begin exploring.

VI
Choose Your Clan

Freya will remind you about the alliances. Seek out one of the three clan bases and speak to the leader. You may also reject all clans and walk as a Scavenger — but know that bosses are far harder without a team at your back.

Bloodcrest Nighthowl Emberwing Scavenger
VII
Begin the Quest for the Keys

Daily and weekly quests will appear as you progress. Complete clan objectives, defeat bosses, explore ancient civilizations. Each key you gather brings the gate to the final darkness one step closer to opening. Collect all 12.

Survival Wisdom

Things Every Traveler Must Know

🌧
Random Events Fire Often

Acid rain, death rain, meteor showers, and more will strike without warning. When you see an announcement, find shelter or seize the opportunity — some events open rare passages.

🏛
Explore Every Civilization

Each ancient region has unique NPC quests, cultural music, and hidden key fragments. Enter cinematic zones slowly — you don't want to miss a single line of voiced dialogue.

🤝
Clans Can Betray Too

The story includes clan spies and political betrayals. Be mindful of who you share information with. Some of your clanmates may not be who they appear.

⚠
Hunting Night Is Not Optional

When the story triggers Hunting Night, underground 3D ghost mobs emerge. They one-shot kill. They do not drop loot. But they are part of the story — the world is becoming more corrupt. Survive it.
