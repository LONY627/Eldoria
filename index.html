The Legend

Once, There Was Peace

L

ong, long ago there stood a kingdom known as Eldoria — where grass and flowers bloomed freely, where the sky shone with rainbow light and the sun blessed every corner of the land. Citizens lived without fear, under the watch of a single, true king: Maximus, brave and honest, who kept the world from the shadows of war for years uncounted.

— Then came the Darkness —

A hidden veil was torn open and all that was peaceful began to decay. King Maximus fought war after war. With the armies of Elves, Humans, Knights, Angels, Romans, and Egyptians at his side, the light of peace grew visible once more... until greed began to stir within the light itself. Traitors rose. Kingdoms shattered. And one dark night, a blade from within his own kingdom silenced the king forever.

· · · ·

Now the sky is black with smoke from burning villages. Rivers run red. The clans that remain blame each other for what was lost. And in the absence of walls and watchful eyes — the monsters walk freely.

Choose Your Allegiance

The Three Clans

Each clan was forged from the ashes of the old kingdom. They fight, they scheme, they survive — and they despise one another. Choose wisely. Your clan is your family, your shield, and your burden.

☠
Bloodcrest

"We were betrayed first. We will strike last."

Warriors hardened by the fall of Eldoria. They believe strength alone determines truth. Fierce in battle, unforgiving in memory. Bloodcrest fights not for glory — but for vengeance.

❍
Nighthowl

"In shadows, truth survives."

Masters of the dark hours and keepers of forbidden knowledge. They distrust daylight and deals alike. Nighthowl moves unseen, gathering secrets and waiting for the moment the world is ready to listen.

𓆰𓆪
Emberwing

"From ruin, we rise again."

Descendants of the old kingdom's builders. They believe Eldoria can be restored. Emberwing seeks the 12 Keys not for power, but to seal the darkness away once and for all and rebuild what was taken.

Or walk alone as a Scavenger — no loyalty, no protection, no mercy.

The Quest

The Twelve Keys

Scattered across ancient ruins, hidden in boss lairs, locked behind riddles and betrayals. Only when all twelve keys are gathered can the dark veil be unsealed. Only then can the final war begin.
