Welcome to Eldoria — Server Status & What to Expect
The server is currently in active development. The world is being built, NPCs are being voiced, and the main story arc is being placed piece by piece. Early players who join now will be among the first to experience the story as it unfolds in real time. Thank you for your patience and your passion — this is being built with nothing but love for the craft.

Current Phase: Early World Development  ·  Server IP: play.eldoria.net  ·  Version: 1.20.x
Recent Dispatches
Update · Volume VII
The Egyptian Region — Foundations Laid
The first ancient civilization zone has been placed in the world. The desert kingdom is taking shape — pyramids, sand-worn temples, and the first hidden chamber containing a key fragment are now accessible. Quests from the local NPCs will arrive in the next build. Cultural music for this region has been composed and is being mixed.
Update · Volume VI
Freya's Voice — Casting Complete
We have completed voice casting for Freya, the player's personal guide. Her lines for the first 3 chapters of the main story have been recorded and will be integrated into the next server build. The world narrator (deep male voice) has also completed his first recording session. Expect both voices to be live very soon.
Update · Volume V
Clan System Goes Live — Bloodcrest, Nighthowl, Emberwing
The three-clan political system is now active on the server. Each clan has an admin-controlled leader who will push the story forward. Clan bases have been constructed. PvP zones outside the main bases are now enabled. Clan quests are being written and will be released in batches. Remember: choosing your alliance carefully could determine your survival.
Update · Volume IV
Custom Mob Pack #1 Released — 38 New Creatures
The first custom mob pack is live. 38 unique creatures have been added to the world, each with custom 3D textures, special attack mechanics, and lore-appropriate names. Boss variants of five of these creatures have also been placed in the world — each with a chance to drop rare key fragments or unique gear. More packs are in development.
Update · Volume III
Random World Events — Now Active
Random events are now firing in the world: Death Rain, Acid Rain, Meteor Showers, and climate disasters have all been activated. Some events are purely atmospheric; others open underground passages or spawn rare encounters. The radio system (/music) is also now working — tune in during events for reactive ambient sound.
Update · Volume II
The Spawn Area — Complete
The spawn area is fully constructed. The cinematic introduction sequence plays automatically for new players. The starting gear set has been finalized. The teleportation NPC and the introductory Quest Board are in place. New players will now experience the intended first ten minutes of Eldoria's story from the moment they log in.
Update · Volume I
Eldoria Opens Its Gates
The server is live. The world is enormous, and still unfinished — but the bones are there. The main story path exists. The first quests have been placed. And somewhere out in the dark, six of the twelve keys are already hidden. Welcome to Eldoria. Try not to die on your first night.

More scrolls will be added as the realm grows. Check back often.
